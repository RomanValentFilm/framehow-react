WORKFLOW

From the storyboard picture extraction, through RECCE workflow, to the final shooting order — step by step.

Load storyboards from PDFs, images, or start drawing from scratch.

EXTRACT storyboard pictures from PDF

Framehow automatically extracts frames from the PDF, including frame numbers and text descriptions. You can manually adjust the results if needed.

Storyboard loads into 3x2 view

Change frame numbers, edit text descriptions, or choose from the available actions for each frame.

SCRIBBLE all over

Used to scribbling all over your PDF? Go ahead — even though in FRAMEHOW each frame is editable on its own.

Rearrange frames by drag & drop, rename, or create new ones.

Want to change the story flow? Simply drag a frame to a new position.

On the RECCE: Use your tablet’s/phone’s camera to shoot storyboard angles.

Find the shooting angles for your storyboard frames on the scout / recce. Adjust the shooting ratio and exposure as needed.

Shoot multiple SHOOTING ANGLES for each frame on the recce and decide which to use later.

Capture multiple versions for each frame to compare and refine your creative choices. Rate camera angles or rearrange them by preference.

Draw and write directly into your shooting angles.

SWIPE through your SHOOTING ANGLES directly in your storyboard.

On the shoot or during prep, you can always access the shooting angles linked to each storyboard frame — so you know how you planned the shot, where you want to shoot it, and which versions you intended to capture. Simply swipe through them on your Tablet.

Draw and write

Annotate each of your shots separately during the recce.

SKETCHES, FLOOR PLANS, STAGING IDEAS.

Draw floor plans, camera moves, staging ideas, and actor positions in a shot.

SKETCHES

Each sketch is linked to a specific frame in the storyboard.

Work with REFERENCES for your shots.

Load references. Just drop in the pictures you want to find instantly while browsing your shots — moods, props, or post-production ideas.

Create SETUPS to visually differentiate your shots.

Assign shots to camera setups, mark them by location, gear, or your own categories, and link specific references to selected shots.

Each frame has its NEEDS

Mark frames by categories: shooting day, unit, location, shooting direction, talent names, gear, post-production process… Rename each category to fit what your shoot needs.

NEEDS strip

Rename & edit each frame’s categories in a side-by-side view.

Overview the STORY FLOW of all frames at once.

Rearrange frames manually, add scene breaks, mark where scenes start or end, or where you change to another location.

SORT frames into SHOOTING ORDERS, make SCHEDULES.

Frames can be automatically sorted by the categories you created into shooting orders. Select the shot continuity for DAY 2, or build the schedule around an actor’s availability. You decide the criteria your shots are ordered by.

SHOOTING ORDER overview, BREAKS, COMPANY MOVES, etc.

Sort automatically, rearrange manually. See what each frame needs, preferred shooting angles, floor plans, breaks, and company moves. Or simply move shots by hand.

GROUP your frames by CUTDOWNS, SCENES, or LOCATIONS.

Make groups of frames that belong to another edit, a shorter story, a location, or a scene. View only frames from selected groups, arrange shooting orders for 2nd Unit, or show the editor which shots belong in the cutdown.

NOTES and CELL TABLES

Create customisable cells and notes associated with each frame.

EXPORT and SHARE… PDFs, KEYNOTE, single IMAGES.

Be prepared on set. Create boards with all the info needed for the shoot, share shooting angles with crew members, photograph props for the set build, or send references for the shots.

Find your preferred EXPORT LAYOUT.

Select only the shots you want to share… sketches of all camera positions, or only the final selected shooting angles, drawings or versions. The choice is yours.

SYNCHRONISATION

It all syncs between your laptop, tablet, and phone. Work offline and Framehow remembers your progress, then uploads it once you’re online again. Editable across all your devices.
