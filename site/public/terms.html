What this is

Framehow is a storyboarding tool, in beta. You may use it free of charge while the beta lasts. It is offered as it is, without a promise that it will be free of faults or available at all times.

Your account

You need an account to keep your work in the cloud. Keep your password to yourself; you are responsible for what is done with your account.

Your work belongs to you

Everything you make in Framehow — your storyboards, pictures, drawings and notes — stays yours. We store it so you can use it across your devices, and we do not use it for anything else.

Storage

Each account has a storage limit during the beta. When it is full, nothing is lost on your device, but new pictures cannot be uploaded until you free space.

Deleting

You can delete your account at any time in Account settings. Everything is kept for seven days in case it was a mistake, and is then erased for good: projects, pictures, restore points and the account itself.

Ending it

You may stop using Framehow whenever you like. We may close an account that is used to break the law or to harm others, and will say why where we can.
