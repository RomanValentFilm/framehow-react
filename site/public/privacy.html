Framehow Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Framehow is operated by Tinge Films s.r.o., Czech Republic, contact: info@framehow.com.

We may store your name, email address, account information, projects, images, notes and basic technical information needed to operate and secure Framehow.

We use this information to provide your account, store and display your projects, operate and improve Framehow, provide support and communicate important changes.

Private project content is not sold or used for advertising or general AI training without separate permission.

Storage

Each account includes a limited amount of storage, shown in the app. We may change the storage limit.

We generally keep account and project data while your account is active. Framehow is not intended to be the only permanent storage location for important files, so users should keep their own backups.

If you delete your account, your data will be removed from active systems within a reasonable period. Temporary backup copies may remain until the normal backup cycle expires.

Inactive beta accounts may also be deleted after reasonable notice.

If Framehow permanently closes, stored projects and user data may be deleted after the announced closure period.

Service Providers

Framehow may use third-party providers for hosting, storage, authentication, email and analytics. We do not sell personal data.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

For privacy questions or requests, contact info@framehow.com.

We may update this Privacy Policy as Framehow develops and will communicate material changes where appropriate.
