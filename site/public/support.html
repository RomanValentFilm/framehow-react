Framehow Support

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to info@framehow.com. We answer as soon as we can.

Common questions

I forgot my password.

On the Log in box, tap "Forgot password?". You get a mail with a link to set a new one.

Where is my work stored?

On Framehow's server and on your device. When you log in and open a project, Framehow loads it onto that device. Framehow is a working tool, not an archive: keep your own copies of important originals.

I deleted a project by mistake.

Open Menu → Open Project and press "Edit Projects". A deleted project is shown in grey for 7 days, with a "Recover" button.

Can I work without a connection?

Yes. Framehow keeps working without a connection. Your changes are saved on your device and sent by themselves as soon as you are online again. This uses your device's own storage, so keep a little space free.

How do I delete my account?

Menu → Account → Delete account. For 7 days it can be restored by logging in again.
