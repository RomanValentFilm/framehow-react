TWO KINDS OF CARD: (a) a MAIN card with a plain photo (no drawing mode) is an <img> whose src is THE WHOLE PHOTO WRITTEN INTO THE PAGE'S HTML (render.ts 700/876, overview.ts 102/472/978), decoding="sync", scaled by the browser — so it is as sharp as the screen allows (up to the photo's 2400 px), and EVERY whole-page redraw writes every main photo (1–1.6 MB of text each) into the HTML again and unpacks each one at full size, the page waiting: 33 shots = 9 s on the iPhone (HDR photos, ~270–750 ms each), 28 shots = 400–900 ms on the iPad. (b) every VERSION card (ANGLE / SKETCH / REFS / LOOKS), and a main card in drawing mode, is a canvas of the shot's own size — 960×540 (540×960 upright) unless cropped — painted from the full photo by drawing.ts paint(); it never shows more than 960 px, on any screen. drawing.ts keeps up to 150 unpacked FULL photos (_decoded; 13 MB each unpacked) — the device throws them out under pressure and unpacks again, which is why it gets slower the more photos there are.
