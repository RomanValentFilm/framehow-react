Privacy Policy

Framehow beta · last changed 22 September 2026
This text is a placeholder for the beta. The final wording is being written.

What we keep

Your name, your email address, an optional profession, and your password in a form that cannot be read back. Plus the work you make: projects, pictures, drawings and notes.

How the app is used

We record which parts of the app are used and from which kind of device, so we can see what works and what does not. This is tied to your account while the account exists.

Who else sees it

Nobody. Your work is not shared, not sold, and not used to train anything. The app runs on Cloudflare, which stores the data on our behalf.

How long

As long as you have an account. When you delete it, everything is kept for seven days and then erased for good — including the record of how you used the app. A deleted project on its own is likewise kept for seven days.

Your rights

You can see and change your name and profession in Account settings, and delete everything at any time. Write to us for anything else.
